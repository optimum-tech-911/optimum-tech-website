## Pixel scenery, cars and supporting cast — 2026-11-05

Nine original raster pixel-art scenes, twelve car models in two views, and seven supporting identities with four poses each were created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool for this game. Full generation/edit prompts, source hashes and framing notes are recorded in `assets/rush/monaco-backgrounds-generation-v1.json`, `assets/rush/pixel-cars/generation-v1.json` and `assets/rush/npc-generation-v2.json`.

Architectural reference sources: [Ports de Monaco — Port Hercule](https://www.ports-monaco.com/en/the-ports-of-monaco/port-hercule/), [Prince's Palace](https://www.palais.mc/en/museum-and-visits/the-state-apartments-1-26.html), [Monte-Carlo SBM — Casino](https://www.montecarlosbm.com/en/casino-monaco/casino-monte-carlo), and the official sources recorded in the scenery manifest. The Casino composition uses its official exterior photograph as an architectural reference. These are artistic Monaco game scenes with compressed geography, not surveyed reconstructions.

- Stage5 Grand Prix (15.4): original circuit geometry, following camera, rival simulation and code-native course artwork. Existing Monaco textures and car sprites are reused. Mini Motor Racing, Horizon Chase2, Rush Rally Origins and art of rally were studied as design references only; their artwork, tracks, audio and code are not shipped. Official source links and the distinction between observation and design inference are in RESEARCH.md.
